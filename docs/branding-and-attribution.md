# Branding and attribution

Product Check follows the EnWithAI Engineering Commons attribution standard (v1.0, adopted 2026-10-10), the same
as Impact Check, Spec Check and Outcome Check.

- **Publisher and copyright holder:** EnWithAI Technologies (the holder name used across the Commons).
- **Names:** used without ™ or ® (governance decision HR-6: none of the names is registered). Product names are
  descriptive and used as "EnWithAI Product Check" where context needs it.
- **License:** MIT (decided 2026-10-12), as for the other Commons tools.
- **README footer:** one footer, at the end, in the shared structure.
- **Tool output:** a one-line generation note in Markdown and dashboard output that `--no-branding` removes. The
  license would not require it.

## Frameworks

| Framework                                     | Owner                  | Relationship   | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CLEAR (CLEAR CT, "Customer Technology Trust") | NetworkGain Consulting | `inspired`     | The design of the assessment model (separate pillars, lifecycle tiers, points of presence, evidence statuses, non-averaging, hard gates overriding scores) was inspired by the CLEAR CT method (v3.1, internal and unpublished). No text, catalog or prompt from it is reproduced; there is no structural relationship. |
| TRACE                                         | EnWithAI Technologies  | `not-assessed` | Product Check's traceability chain may match TRACE's intent, but no TRACE specification is available to assess it.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

Upgrading either relationship requires a versioned, publishable specification and the owner's approval (see the
Commons `FRAMEWORK_PROVENANCE.md`).

## Wording that is deliberately not used

"CLEAR-certified", "TRACE-compliant", "powered by CLEAR", "official implementation", "production-ready guarantee",
"penetration tested", "compliant". The brief's suggested attribution sentence ("informed by CLEAR™ … aligned with
TRACE™ …") was adapted: no ™ symbols (HR-6), and "aligned with TRACE" is not claimed without a specification.
